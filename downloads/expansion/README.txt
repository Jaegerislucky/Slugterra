SLUGTERRA: CLASH + EXPLORATION UPDATE

INSTALL
Stop Play and save a backup. Open Install-Expansion.txt, copy ALL its contents,
paste into Studio's Command Bar, and press Enter. Look for EXPANSION INSTALLED.
No mesh imports are needed. This targets the existing simulator economy,
humanoid trainers, fixed battle positions and capsule-hotbar version from chat.

CONTROLS
Hold LEFT SHIFT to sprint; release to walk. Shift Lock is disabled to avoid a
conflict. FAST TRAVEL opens a list of unlocked destinations. UPGRADES opens
coin-funded upgrades; the existing capsule shop also links to that window.

A trainer fight now uses a clash: both slugs launch in cute form and transform.
Tap the button or press SPACE repeatedly during the five-second timer. Reach the
shown tap target to win. Targets increase from 20 to 35 across the six areas.
The server validates timing, the round token, ownership and a maximum accepted
rate of twelve taps per second. Scores are not accepted from the client.
Damage and rewards apply only after the ending animation, with floating HP text.

UPGRADES
Damage: +10% base damage per level, cost 100 * (next level)^2.
Coins: +5% battle coins per level, cost 150 * (next level)^2.
Speed: +0.5 walking/+0.4 sprint speed per level, cost 100 * (next level)^2.
Maximum level: 20. Coin and damage displays include the upgrades.
Upgrades remain session-only, like the current inventory/coin progression.

NEW AREAS
Mossy Hollows, Storm Grotto and Astral Depths extend the map to six areas.
Win trainer fights to unlock them. Fast travel rejects locked destinations.
Wild-slug and hourly event spawning is expanded to all six sections.

AUDIO / FX
Adds bundled synthetic creature/power-up cues, clash charge effects, dual
projectiles and impacts, plus rarity-colored confetti on capsule reveal.
Bundled sounds are placeholders, not custom recorded animal/blaster assets.
Existing imported blaster and visible chamber slug are retained. The chamber
waits for the clash to finish before reloading when its service is installed.

GAME PASSES
Deferred as requested. ExpansionConfig.Passes is empty; no pass buttons or
purchases are enabled. No IDs are required for this update.
